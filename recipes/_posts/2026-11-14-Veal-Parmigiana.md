{: .box-note}
**Serves:** 6<br>
**Prep:** 20 min, **Total:** 45 min

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :------ |:--- |
| Egg | 1 |
| Water | 2 tbsp |
| Unseasoned Dry Bread Crumbs | 2/3 Cup |
| Grated Parmesan Cheese | 1/3 Cup |
| Veal Leg Cutlets, 1/4 inch thick | 1 1/2 lb |
| Olive or Vegetable Oil | 1/4 Cup |
| Tomato Pasta Sauce | 1 Cup |
| Shredded Mozzarella Cheese | 2 Cups (8 oz) |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350F. Beat the egg and water in a small bowl. Mix the bread crumbs and Parmesan in a shallow dish.

2. Dip each cutlet in the egg, then coat it in the crumb mixture.

3. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Cook half the veal about 5 minutes, turning once, until light brown. Drain, then do the rest with the other 2 tbsp of oil.

4. Lay the veal in an ungreased 13x9-inch glass baking dish, overlapping the slices a little. Spoon the sauce over it and top with the mozzarella.

5. Bake uncovered about 25 minutes, until the sauce is bubbly and the cheese is light brown.

## No Cutlets?

1 1/2 lb of veal round steak works too. Cut it into 12 pieces, put them between sheets of plastic wrap or waxed paper, and pound them to 1/4 inch thick with the flat side of a meat mallet.

## Chicken Parmigiana

Swap the veal for 6 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1 1/2 lb). Pound each one, smooth side down, between plastic wrap or waxed paper to 1/4 inch thick. In step 3, cook the chicken until it's no longer pink in the center.
