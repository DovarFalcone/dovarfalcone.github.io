{: .box-note}
**Serves:** 4<br>
**Prep:** 10 min, **Total:** 40 min

Bulgogi is thin-sliced beef in a sweet and salty soy marinade, cooked fast in a hot pan.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :------ |:--- |
| Boneless Beef Top Loin or Sirloin Steak | 1 lb |
| Soy Sauce | 1/4 Cup |
| Sugar | 3 tbsp |
| Sesame or Vegetable Oil | 2 tbsp |
| Pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Green Onions, finely chopped | 3 Medium (3 tbsp) |
| Garlic, chopped | 2 Cloves |
| Hot Cooked Rice | For serving |

## Instructions

1. Slice the beef diagonally across the grain into 1/8-inch strips. It's much easier to cut thin if it's partly frozen.

2. In a medium glass or plastic bowl, mix everything except the beef and rice. Add the beef and stir until it's well coated.

3. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

4. Drain the beef and throw out the marinade.

5. Heat a 10-inch skillet over medium heat. Cook the beef 2 to 3 minutes, stirring often, until brown.

> Serve over rice. Kimchi on the side is worth it if you like heat, and most grocery stores carry it.
