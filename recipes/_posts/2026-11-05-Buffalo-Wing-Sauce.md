> I make this one a lot, and it's the sauce people ask about. The original is the [Buffalo Chicken Wing Sauce on Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/219109/buffalo-chicken-wing-sauce/).

Makes enough for about 8 servings of wings.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :------ |:--- |
| Frank's RedHot | 2/3 Cup |
| Butter | 1/2 Cup |
| White Vinegar | 1 1/2 tbsp |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 1/4 tsp |
| Cayenne Pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Garlic Powder | 1/8 tsp |
| Salt | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Put everything in a small saucepan over medium heat.

2. Whisk the whole time as it heats up.

3. Once it starts bubbling around the edges, pull it off the heat.

4. Give it one more whisk and set it aside until the wings are ready.

Enjoy!
