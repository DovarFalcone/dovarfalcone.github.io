> I make this one a lot, and it's the sauce people ask about. It started as the [Buffalo Chicken Wing Sauce on Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/219109/buffalo-chicken-wing-sauce/). I add brown sugar and a little honey to round out the heat.

Makes enough for about 8 servings of wings.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :------ |:--- |
| Frank's RedHot | 2/3 Cup |
| Butter | 1/2 Cup |
| White Vinegar | 1 1/2 tbsp |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 1/4 tsp |
| Cayenne Pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Garlic Powder | 1/8 tsp |
| Brown Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Honey | 1 tsp |
| Salt | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Put everything in a small saucepan over medium heat.

2. Whisk the whole time as it heats up.

3. Once it starts bubbling around the edges, pull it off the heat.

4. Give it one more whisk and set it aside until the wings are ready.

## Crispy Wing Tips

Don't throw out the wing tips. They're mostly skin and cartilage, so in the air fryer at high heat they crisp up like cracklings and hold the sauce well.

### Prep

1. Pat the tips completely dry with paper towels. Any moisture left on them keeps them from crisping.

2. Toss them in a bowl with a drizzle of high-heat oil, like avocado or vegetable oil.

3. Season with salt, garlic powder, and onion powder. Skip the baking powder or cornstarch. There's so much skin that they crisp fine without it.

### Air Fry

1. Preheat the air fryer to 400F.

2. Lay the tips in a single layer in the basket. Don't crowd them, and work in batches if you need to.

3. Cook 20 to 25 minutes, shaking the basket every 7 to 8 minutes. They're done when they're dark golden brown and rigid.

### Sauce

1. While they cook, warm up a batch of the sauce above in a large bowl. In a pinch, 2 parts Frank's to 1 part melted unsalted butter works too.

2. Drop the tips straight from the air fryer into the bowl and toss until coated.

> Serve right away with celery and blue cheese or ranch. You crunch right through the skin and cartilage and leave only the little center bone.

Enjoy!
